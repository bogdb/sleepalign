A letter made of flowers, for a baby born in November.

Every letter of the alphabet, A to Z, drawn in soft pink and mauve chrysanthemums with sage leaves, in a watercolour style on a warm paper background. The chrysanthemum is November's birth flower, and the small line under each letter reads "November · Chrysanthemum".

Hang the letters of the name at the baby shower, then frame the first one for the nursery.

This is an original digital design, not a copy of anyone else's artwork. I designed it with the help of AI and digital tools. It is a digital download: you print it at home or at a print shop. Nothing is posted to you.

WHAT YOU GET

All 26 letters, A to Z, in five print sizes:
- 5x7 in (plus a set without the caption, for banners)
- 8x10 in
- 11x14 in
- A4
- A3

Every file is a high resolution JPG at 300 dpi, so it prints sharp at its full size. You also get a one-page printing guide.

The files come as five zip folders, one per size. They appear on your Etsy downloads page as soon as payment goes through.

MAKING THE NAME BANNER

1. Open the 5x7 folder marked "no-caption".
2. Print the letters of the name, one per sheet.
3. Trim, punch two small holes, and thread them on twine or ribbon.

Any name works, because every letter is included. Print a letter twice if the name needs it.

PRINTING TIPS

- Print at 100% or "actual size". Turn off "fit to page".
- Matte card or fine art paper (200 to 300 gsm) looks closest to a painting.
- Home printers are fine up to A4. For 11x14 and A3, use a print shop or an online print service.
- Colours can look slightly different on screen and on paper. That's normal.

FRAMES ARE NOT INCLUDED. The photos show styling ideas.
